Undergraduate Student · Materials Science & Engineering. University of Rajshahi

I’m an undergraduate student in Materials Science and Engineering at the University of Rajshahi (2025-2029). I’m interested in how processing and structure shape the properties of materials, and in the characterization techniques, such as XRD and electron microscopy, used to study them. I also use Python and C to automate analysis and explore computational approaches to materials problems. I’m looking for opportunities to get hands-on experience through research projects, lab work and collaboration.
